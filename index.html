Good questions

Everything you're wondering

Do I qualify for GLP-1 medication?
Eligibility depends on your BMI, health history, and a clinical evaluation. The 60-second questionnaire gives you an initial answer; your provider makes the final call after reviewing your labs.
Why do you require labs first?
Because prescribing without data isn't medicine. A baseline panel confirms treatment is safe for you and gives us the numbers to measure real progress — beyond the scale.
What does it cost?
One transparent monthly price per program, published on our pricing page. No hidden compounding fees, no gated memberships.
Will I keep seeing the same provider?
Yes. Continuity of care is the whole point. You'll build your plan with the same clinician at every check-in.
What happens when I hit my goal?
You move into maintenance with your same provider — and since we're also your primary care home, your care simply continues.

Weight loss questions, answered

Which medications do you offer?
Your provider will discuss semaglutide and tirzepatide options — including formulation and sourcing — and recommend what fits your biology and budget. Nothing is prescribed before a licensed clinician evaluates you.
Will I lose muscle?
Rapid weight loss can cost lean mass, which is why protein and strength guidance is built into every plan rather than sold separately.
How fast will I see results?
Appetite changes typically begin within weeks; meaningful, sustainable results build over months of consistent care and titration. Your provider sets honest expectations at your first visit.
What if it's not right for me?
Then we'll tell you — and talk through what is. Eligibility screening is free, and our providers decline to prescribe when it isn't clinically appropriate.
